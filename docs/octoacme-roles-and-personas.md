# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons bridge communication between internal project teams and external stakeholders. They ensure stakeholder needs are understood, expectations are managed, and feedback flows effectively in both directions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document stakeholder requirements and concerns
- Provide regular status updates and progress reports to stakeholders
- Manage stakeholder expectations regarding scope, timeline, and deliverables
- Facilitate communication between stakeholders and the project team
- Address stakeholder feedback and escalate issues as needed
- Document stakeholder decisions and change requests

### Goals
- Maintain strong stakeholder relationships and confidence
- Ensure stakeholder needs are reflected in project planning
- Minimize misalignment and scope disputes
- Enable timely feedback loops for course corrections

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder status briefings and review meetings
- Requirements documentation and change request forms
- Escalation reports when conflicts arise
- Feedback synthesis and summary documents

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Works closely with Project Manager** on timeline and scope communication
- **Collaborates with Product Manager** on requirements and prioritization feedback
- **Partners with Executive Sponsor** on strategic alignment and high-level decisions
- **Coordinates with Development Team** to communicate technical constraints and feasibility

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and oversee quality standards, testing protocols, and quality gates throughout the project lifecycle. They ensure deliverables meet quality criteria and mitigate risks related to defects and technical debt.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria for deliverables
- Create and maintain test plans and test strategies
- Execute quality gates and ensure compliance with standards
- Report on quality metrics, defects, and testing progress
- Identify and escalate quality risks
- Recommend process improvements to prevent defects
- Coordinate with development and operations on quality assurance activities

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable software and services
- Minimize post-launch defects and customer issues
- Improve overall quality metrics and team quality awareness
- Enable confident releases with measurable quality indicators

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and quality gate reports
- Defect logs and quality dashboards
- Test execution summaries and coverage reports
- Quality retrospectives and improvement recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Collaborates with Developers** on test requirements and quality standards
- **Partners with Project Manager** on quality timeline and resource planning
- **Works with Product Manager** on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- **Coordinates with Release Manager** on quality sign-offs before deployment

---

## Process Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Process Compliance Officers ensure the project adheres to established organizational processes, governance standards, and regulatory requirements. They promote process consistency, identify deviations, and recommend improvements to strengthen project execution.

### Responsibilities
- Monitor project execution against defined processes and standards
- Document and escalate process deviations and non-compliance issues
- Conduct process audits and effectiveness reviews
- Recommend process improvements based on lessons learned
- Ensure proper documentation and audit trail maintenance
- Communicate process standards and expectations to the team
- Support compliance with organizational and regulatory requirements

### Goals
- Ensure consistent application of project management best practices
- Minimize process-related risks and inefficiencies
- Build organizational maturity and process discipline
- Support regulatory compliance and audit readiness

### Typical Communication
- Compliance audit reports and deviation logs
- Process improvement recommendations
- Governance and standards documentation
- Audit trail and documentation summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Program Manager** on compliance status and organizational alignment
- **Coordinates with Project Manager** on process adherence and documentation
- **Works with all project roles** to communicate standards and gather feedback
- **Partners with Quality Assurance Lead** on quality process compliance

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define the technical strategy, design decisions, and architectural direction for projects. They ensure technical solutions align with organizational standards, scale requirements, and long-term technology roadmap.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical strategy and architectural approach
- Create and review technical specifications and design documents
- Conduct architecture and design reviews
- Provide technical guidance and mentorship to development teams
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Ensure alignment with organizational technology standards
- Support technology decisions and trade-off analysis

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and robust technical solutions
- Reduce technical risk and minimize rework
- Ensure consistency with organizational technology direction
- Enable knowledge transfer and technical team capability growth

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design documents and technical specifications
- Design review meeting notes and decisions
- Technical risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Technology selection rationale and trade-off analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Developers** on technical implementation and design guidance
- **Collaborates with Project Manager** on technical timeline and complexity estimation
- **Works with Product Manager** on feasibility analysis and technical trade-offs
- **Coordinates with Operations** on deployment, scalability, and operational requirements
